Soul Tabletop: tabletop directions

Same Systems pages in each direction. Pick a direction, then try the navigation toggle and the table/cards switch in the list toolbar. Each list page has its own default view, and people can switch it. Buttons are solid, every text color passes WCAG AA on its background, and there's one light theme.

A · Vellum

Warm parchment, ink-brown text, oxblood primary, moss green for secondary accents. Cormorant Garamond headings, Source Sans body. A rulebook printed on good paper.

B · Map Room

Cool sage paper, deep teal primary, brass accents, crisp corners. Cinzel inscriptional headings, Alegreya Sans body. An explorer's chart table.

C · Grimoire

A bright, nearly white page, deep indigo ink, gilt accents, softer rounded corners. EB Garamond headings, Nunito Sans body. The lightest touch of the three.

D · Folio

Between A and C: a lightly warm page with white panels, plum (where oxblood meets indigo), gilt accents. Cormorant Garamond headings with C's Nunito Sans body, medium corners.
